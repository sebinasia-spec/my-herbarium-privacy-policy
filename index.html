Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version: My Herbarium doesn't have an account system, doesn't track you, and doesn't run a server of its own. Everything you log stays on your device. If you buy an optional plant pack, a purchase service called RevenueCat keeps a record of that purchase, and nothing else.

At a glance

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing tied to your identity.
  • Your garden stays on your device. Your logged plants, custom entries, and settings are stored locally, not on our servers.
  • No analytics, no ad trackers. We don't watch how you use the app.
  • Nothing is sold. We don't sell data, and your garden is never shared. The only outside service the app talks to about you is RevenueCat, and only for plant pack purchases.
  • Plant packs are optional. They're paid add-ons handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • You're in control of sharing. The Share feature only sends anything anywhere when you explicitly choose to.

What the app stores

My Herbarium keeps a few things in local storage on your device, so the app works and remembers your progress between sessions:

  • The plants you've logged, and when you logged them
  • Any custom plants you've added yourself
  • Your preferences — scoring mode, which day your week starts on, sound on or off
  • Whether you've been through the onboarding walkthrough
  • Which plant packs you own, so they still work without a connection

None of your logged plants, custom entries, or preferences is sent to us, to advertisers, or to anyone else. It's not linked to your name, email, or any other identity — the app doesn't ask for or know any of those in the first place. It simply lives in your device's storage, the same way a note-taking app remembers your notes.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your location
  • Camera, microphone, or photo library access
  • Your contacts
  • Analytics or usage tracking
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Cookies or web trackers
  • Your payment card or bank details

There are no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs built into the app. The one third-party service built in is RevenueCat, which is used only for purchases and is described in the next section.

Plant packs and purchases

My Herbarium is free to use. Optional plant packs add extra plants to your catalogue and are bought as one-off purchases through Google Play or the App Store.

Your payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or bank details, your name, or your email address.

To unlock a pack you've bought, and to bring it back if you reinstall the app or change phone, My Herbarium uses RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app purchases for us. Each time the app opens, it may contact RevenueCat to check which packs your store account owns. This can happen even if you've never bought anything. RevenueCat receives:

  • A randomly generated, anonymous ID for your installation. It isn't your name, your email, or an account with us.
  • Your purchase history for this app: which packs you've bought, with the receipts and times.
  • The technical details that come with any connection to an online service, such as the app version, your platform and operating system version, your language, region and currency, and your device's IP address.

RevenueCat uses this to confirm purchases with Google or Apple, unlock your packs, prevent fraud, and let us see how many packs have been bought. It isn't used for advertising or to follow you across other apps. RevenueCat doesn't receive your location, your contacts, or anything from your plant log. How RevenueCat handles data is described in its own privacy policy.

The app keeps a copy of which packs you own on your device, so your packs keep working when you're offline.

Sharing your garden

When you tap Share, My Herbarium draws an image of your week's specimens right there on your device and hands it to your phone's native share sheet. We never see that image or receive a copy — it only goes wherever you choose to send it, exactly like sharing a photo from your camera roll.

Emailing us about a missing plant

If you use the "missing a plant?" link in the Almanac, it opens your own email app with a message pre-addressed to us. We only receive what you choose to write and send — the link itself never sends anything for you.

App updates

My Herbarium checks Expo's update service (the infrastructure we use to publish the app) for new versions, the same way any app checks for updates. This exchange involves standard technical details like app and platform version, and a random token that tells the service whether your installation has already asked for an update. If a downloaded update ever fails to start, the app also tells the service which update it was, so a working one can be sent instead. None of this includes anything from your plant log or any personal data.

Downloading the app itself happens through the App Store or Google Play, who handle that delivery and any diagnostics under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Your garden lives on your device and nowhere else, so deleting the app deletes your logged plants, custom entries, and settings with it. There's no copy on a server for us to hold onto.

The record of which packs you've bought is different. Google or Apple holds it as the store, and RevenueCat holds it under the anonymous ID described above. Deleting the app doesn't remove it, because it's what lets you restore a pack later. If you'd like us to ask RevenueCat to erase it, email us. Because the ID is anonymous, we may ask for the order number from your store receipt to find the right record. Your purchase itself stays with your Google or Apple account.

Your rights

Regulations like the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, export, and delete your personal data. We don't hold personal data on a server of our own, and your garden is already entirely in your hands, on your device. The only record about you held elsewhere is the anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat. If you'd like a copy of it, or want it erased, email us and we'll take it up with RevenueCat for you.

Children's privacy

My Herbarium doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The app doesn't ask for a name, email, or any account. Purchases are made through your Google Play or App Store account, so the store's own parental controls apply to them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — say, if a future version of the app adds a feature that works differently — we'll update the effective date above and describe the change here.
